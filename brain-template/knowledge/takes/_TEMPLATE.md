Two or three lines on what you actually think, and — more useful — what
you think is wrong with the common view.

> VERBATIM: "The sentence as you'd actually say it out loud."

One or two lines on where the position comes from: what you saw, built, or
got burned by that led here. Then stop. This is a retrieval unit, not an
essay.
