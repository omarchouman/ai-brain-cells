The situation that taught it to you — one or two lines, enough to make the
lesson concrete rather than a platitude.

The transferable part: what someone else should do differently, and under
what conditions it applies. Name the conditions. A lesson without a scope
becomes bad advice the moment it's quoted somewhere else.
